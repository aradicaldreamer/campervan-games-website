Merge Kitchen

Our new prototype: a cosy merge-and-cook game about travel and good food. Combine ingredients on the board, cook dishes at your stations, and serve the regulars who drop by your kitchen.

It's an early, playable demo - free, in your browser, best on a phone.

On itch.io

How it's being made

This prototype is a solo project built with AI assistance. The code was written together with Claude, Anthropic's AI model, working from my design and direction, and the placeholder art, characters and icons were generated with generative AI tools. It has been a great way to experiment with new tools and learn what they are good for, and to get a playable idea in front of people quickly.

The full game will be made with 100% human-made assets, on the foundation of the code we've built here. Funding for that version isn't open yet. When it is, we'll link to the pitch materials from this page.

We'd love your feedback while we build it out - drop us a line.
